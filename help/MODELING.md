# Modeling help

Use `stats` (or `std.stats`) for predictive modeling; everything is dependency-free and returns ordinary records. Generalized linear models: `glm(features, target, family, names, confidence, max_iter, tol)` with `"gaussian"`, `"binomial"`, or `"poisson"`; convenience wrappers `logistic_regression` and `poisson_regression`; prediction with `predict_glm`. Regularized regression: `ridge_regression`, `lasso_regression`, `elastic_net`. Survival analysis: `kaplan_meier`, `log_rank_test`, `cox_proportional_hazards`, `concordance_index`. Forecasting: `arima(values, p, d, q, periods, include_mean, max_iter, tol)` and `auto_arima(values, max_p, max_q, d, periods, include_mean, max_iter)`. Record wrappers: `analysis.glm_report`, `analysis.survival_report`, `analysis.arima_report`. See `docs/ADVANCED_MODELING.md`.
